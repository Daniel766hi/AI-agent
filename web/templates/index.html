Fetch prices into the cache

IDX tickers take a .JK suffix; crypto on Yahoo is BTC-USD; CoinGecko wants the slug (bitcoin), not the ticker. Anything fetched is cached and becomes available to Screen and Research.

Screen many assets

Screening N assets with K configs is N×K trials, and at that many attempts something always looks brilliant. Both p-values are shown: per-asset, and charged for the whole screen. Run the synthetic universe at your real screen size to see what your setup produces from noise alone.
